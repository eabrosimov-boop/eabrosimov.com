Overview

Whale season in Puerto Madryn has begun — and we're heading out for an immersive journey through this unique region of ocean and wildlife.

Over five days we'll see the world's largest continental penguin colony, go out to sea on a semi-submarine, search for whales from shore and water, watch the sunset from kayaks, swim with sea lions and meet the largest dinosaurs ever found on Earth. One trip — everything worth coming here for.

Day-by-Day Itinerary

Day 1. Arrival
Trelew → Puerto Madryn

We fly into Trelew, pick up our luggage and head to the coast — about an hour's drive to Puerto Madryn.

We check in, have lunch. We spend the first evening getting to know each other and taking our first photos of whales in the distance, right from the shore. As a bonus — sea lions are often lounging on the pier itself, and you can watch them for as long as you like, completely free. We rest after the flight and get ready for a packed programme tomorrow.

Trelew → Puerto Madryn Distance: ~65 km Travel time: ~1 hour

---

Day 2. Whales

First thing in the morning we head to Puerto Pirámides — this is where all the whale-watching boats depart from. Southern right whales come here from June to December — females arrive to give birth and nurse their calves. Our vessel is a semi-submarine, so we get to watch the whales both from above and directly underwater.

The second half of the day we spend on the Valdés Peninsula — we'll see bird colonies, sea lions, and at high tide we'll try our luck spotting whales right from the shore. Prepare to be amazed.

Puerto Madryn → Puerto Pirámides → Valdés Peninsula → Puerto Madryn

Distance: ~170 km Travel time: ~3.5 hours (with stops)

---

Day 3. Penguins and Kayaks

In the morning we drive to Trelew — to the Egidio Feruglio Palaeontology Museum, one of the best in Latin America. It houses the skeletons of the largest dinosaurs ever found on the planet: Patagotitan, Giganotosaurus — the names speak for themselves. Even if dinosaurs aren't your thing, the scale is impressive.

Next — Punta Tombo. The world's largest continental colony of Magellanic penguins: over half a million birds spread across several kilometres of coastline. The penguins here aren't in an enclosure — they simply live here, build nests and walk the paths alongside people.

In the evening — we try to catch a unique sunset, heading out to sea on kayaks. A short paddle on calm waters to catch the signature Patagonian red sky.

Puerto Madryn → Trelew → Punta Tombo → Puerto Madryn

Distance: ~260 km Travel time: ~4 hours (with stops)

---

Day 4. Snorkelling with Sea Lions

We start the morning with one of the most wild activities on the itinerary — heading out into the ocean to swim with sea lions. Underwater they're completely different — fast, curious, circling all around you.

Afterwards — hotel, shower, lunch. The afternoon is free: we can head back to the shore to watch whales from a deserted beach, stroll along the seafront, or browse for souvenirs at the local fair.

---

Day 5. Departure

Early departure to Trelew airport, flight at 10:00.

Puerto Madryn → Trelew (airport)

Distance: ~65 km Travel time: ~1 hour
