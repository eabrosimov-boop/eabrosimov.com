Patagonia: Argentina & Chile Trekking

⏱️ 13–15 days 📍 Chile · Argentina

Overview

The finest routes of Argentine and Chilean Patagonia in one journey. Torres del Paine, Fitz Roy, Perito Moreno and Tierra del Fuego — without tents, heavy packs or camp life.

Day-by-Day Itinerary

Day 1. Buenos Aires

Arrival at Buenos Aires airport. Transfer to hotel and check-in. Free time and rest after the long flight.

In the evening, meet your group and take a walk through the city at night.

---

Day 2. Discovering the Capital

We explore the beautiful streets of Buenos Aires. The colour, atmosphere and architecture of South America's most European capital.

We see the main Plaza de Mayo, the pink Presidential Palace, the Colón Theatre. We walk through the colonial San Telmo quarter with the colourful houses of La Boca. We visit the modern Puerto Madero neighbourhood, where old architecture harmoniously blends with elite new buildings.

Dinner in an atmospheric restaurant. Night in Buenos Aires.

Buenos Aires — a capital with history and life on the streets

---

Day 3. Ushuaia

Breakfast and flight to the airport. After 3.5 hours we land on Tierra del Fuego in the southernmost city on the planet — Ushuaia (beyond it lies only the ice of Antarctica).

Check-in to the hotel. At 17:00 we set out on a 2-hour boat trip along the Beagle Channel. We see cormorants, sea lions, Arctic birds. If we're lucky — we'll meet a whale. We approach the southernmost lighthouse on Earth — Les Éclaireurs.

Evening dinner at a restaurant with fresh crab meat, fish and seafood.

Tierra del Fuego — the edge of the world with icy seas

---

Day 4. Esmeralda Lagoon

After breakfast we head to Esmeralda Lagoon. The route is about 8 km.

Passing through peat bogs, forest and streams, we reach a lagoon nestled between mountains. We admire the glaciers, take a break and enjoy a snack. We see the massive dams built by engineer beavers.

Around 16:00 we return to Ushuaia, have lunch and rest. The evening is free — you can explore the city or simply relax.

A lagoon among mountains and first serious treks

---

Day 5. Journey to Chile

Long drive on comfortable tourist buses — about 7 hours. We cross the Strait of Magellan by ferry (chance to see dolphins!), then cross into Chile.

The views are breathtaking: endless plains framed by a belt of mountains, wild guanacos and llamas grazing freely.

Night in Punta Arenas. We arrive around 19:00. Dinner and walk along the waterfront.

Crossing the border with views of the Andes

---

Day 6. Punta Arenas. Penguins

Early morning departure from Punta Arenas to see penguins. Short bus ride (40 minutes), then we transfer to a boat and sail to Magdalena Island.

We meet colonies of two species of penguins — Gentoo and Magellanic. We walk among the birds in their natural habitat, take beautiful photos.

We return to the hotel, pack our things and at 13:00 take a transfer to Puerto Natales (2 hours). We check into an atmospheric hotel with hot baths overlooking the Andes.

Meeting penguins and Chilean Patagonia

---

Day 7. Torres del Paine National Park

The park impresses with its diverse landscapes: sparkling ice fields, blue-green lagoons, golden meadows, quiet river villages.

A scenic circular walk through the park. We climb to the most picturesque viewpoints — Mirador Cuernos and Mirador Condor. So many viewing platforms that your phone memory will fill instantly with photos.

Lunch at a restaurant with views of Cuernos mountain. In the evening we return to Puerto Natales to rest in a comfortable hotel.

Torres del Paine — Chile's most beautiful park

---

Day 8. Torres Towers

The Torres Towers are the trademark of the park. A symbol of the conservation of Chile's natural wealth and one of the most impressive places for active tourism and adventure.

A full day hiking trip — 21 km. Several breaks for snacks. Packed lunch included in the tour price.

Late evening return to the hotel, tired but satisfied. Dinner and rest.

The climax of trekking in Patagonia

---

Day 9. El Calafate

Breakfast, we board a public bus and return to Argentina — to El Calafate (6 hours in the car). A small tourist town in the heart of Patagonia.

After lunch and rest we walk in the city national reserve. We see pink flamingos and various exotic birds.

Dinner at an atmospheric steakhouse. Get ready to meet Glacier Perito Moreno!

Return to Argentine Patagonia

---

Day 10. Perito Moreno Glacier

We head to Glacier Perito Moreno. Fantastic spectacle! The glacier constantly advances toward the shore — there's a chance to hear the thunder of breaking ice and see huge ice blocks falling into the water.

We sail on a small boat very close to the glacier. In the afternoon we walk on scenic trails with panoramic views.

Those who wish can walk on the glacier with crampons (approximately $200 extra).

In the evening we drive to El Chaltén (about 3 hours).

Meeting an ice beauty

---

Day 11. Mount Fitz Roy

We climb to a viewpoint with impressive views of the Fitz Roy peaks and surrounding nature. A lake at the base of the mountain.

Trek 22 km, full trip 8-10 hours. Those who are tired stay at the hotel.

El Chaltén is a very atmospheric and cosy little town, reminiscent of a Swiss village. Here you can have a wonderful time.

Upon return, dinner and sharing of impressions.

Fitz Roy — icon of Patagonia

---

Day 12. Buenos Aires

We return by bus to El Calafate (2 hours), from where we fly to Buenos Aires.

We spend free time in the city's parks and squares. Those interested attend a tango lesson.

Farewell dinner in the evening.

Return to the capital before going home
