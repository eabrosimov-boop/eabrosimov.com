Highland Argentina

⏱️ 4 days 📍 Salta, Jujuy

Overview

A flight from Buenos Aires to Salta, then into the high-altitude northwest of Argentina. Villages lost at 4000+ meters, panoramas that redefine your sense of scale. Salt flats where water and salt are one word, canyons and mountain trails. The spirit of Guemes will accompany us — from the airport named after him to the streets of his city and the restaurant where we dine in his honour.

    What to bring

    Essential items:

  • Sunscreen (UV is very strong at altitude)
  • Hat and sunglasses
  • Warm clothing (it gets cold in the mountains even in summer)
  • Plenty of water (2–3 litres per day)
  • Comfortable hiking shoes

    • Important notes:

  • High altitude (3500–4300 m): watch for altitude sickness
  • Acclimatization: take it easy on the first day
  • Water and hydration are key to comfort
  • Tell us immediately if you feel unwell
