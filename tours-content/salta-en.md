# Highland Argentina — Day by Day

## Day 0. Buenos Aires → Salta

**14:15** — Departure from Buenos Aires airport
- Flight JA3010
- Flight time: ~2 hours

**~16:30** — Arrival at Guemes Airport, Salta
- Customs, car rental pickup (~30 minutes)
- Why is the airport, street, and restaurant named after Guemes? We'll learn these stories along the way

**~17:00** — Check-in and rest

**17:30–19:00** — Walking tour through Salta centre to the Guemes statue
- Leisurely stroll down Guemes Street to the city's main monument
- Stories about the legend behind this name

**~20:00** — Dinner at Guemes restaurant
- Get to know each other
- Discuss tomorrow's program
- Rest and prepare for the high-altitude journey ahead

---

## Day 1. Salta → Purmamarca

**Morning at the hotel**
- Breakfast included
- Check out
- Store bags at the hotel

**09:00** — City walk begins

**09:15** — San Bernardo funicular
- Walk to the station (~15 minutes)
- Take the funicular to the viewpoint
- Panoramic view of Salta from above

**~10:30** — MAAM Museum
- Art and culture of the region
- History of indigenous peoples

**12:30** — Lunch at a local restaurant

**14:00** — Drive to Purmamarca
- Distance: ~3 hours
- 1–2 stops at scenic overlooks along the way
- First introduction to the landscapes of the northwest

**~17:30** — Arrival in Purmamarca and hotel check-in

**Evening in Purmamarca**
- Walk to Cerro de los 7 Colores (Hill of Seven Colours) — ~40 minutes on foot
- Local craft shops and artisan markets
- Free time to relax
- Dinner at the hotel or in town

## Day 2. Salinas Grandes → Humahúaca

**08:30** — Breakfast (choice of Patagua or Logros cafés)

**09:25** — Load into cars and depart for Salinas Grandes
- Bring warm clothes and plenty of water!
- Today we ascend above 4000 metres

**11:00–14:30** — Salinas Grandes
- Drive time (round trip): ~3.5 hours (with stops)
- On the salt flats themselves: 1–1.5 hours
- Stops at mountain viewpoints
- Sandwich lunch at altitude

**14:30** — Arrival in Humahúaca and main lunch

**~15:30** — Check-in at Casa de Piedra hotel

**16:00** — Jeep excursion to Hornocal (14 Colours)
- Altitude: ~4300 metres
- Water is mandatory!
- Some of the most beautiful views in Argentina

**~18:30** — Return to Humahúaca

**20:30** — Dinner with live music at Aisito
- Local folk music and traditional cuisine
- Evening in the spirit of the region

---

## Day 3. Humahúaca → Uquía → Purmamarca → Salta

**08:00** — Breakfast at the hotel
- Prepared by a local woman (you can help in the kitchen if you wish)

**09:00–10:30** — City walk (optional)
- Departure time flexible based on group preference

**10:30** — Departure for Uquía (30 minutes)
- All bags packed and ready

**In Uquía**
- Visit the historic church
- Trek through Quebrada de las Señoritas
  - Route: ~3–4 km
  - Duration: 1–1.5 hours
  - Beautiful canyon scenery
  - Bring water!

**13:30** — Departure to Purmamarca (30 minutes)

**14:00–16:00** — Purmamarca: lunch and shopping
- Final walk through the main square
- Local crafts and souvenirs
- Last chance to absorb the atmosphere

**16:00** — Drive back to Salta
- Distance: ~3 hours
- Descent from the highlands to the valley

**~19:00** — Arrival in Salta
- Hotel check-in
- Dinner and rest
- Stories of adventure

## What to bring

**Essential items:**
- Sunscreen (UV is very strong at altitude)
- Hat and sunglasses
- Warm clothing (it gets cold in the mountains even in summer)
- Plenty of water (2–3 litres per day)
- Comfortable hiking shoes

**Important notes:**
- High altitude (3500–4300 m): watch for altitude sickness
- Acclimatization: take it easy on the first day
- Water and hydration are key to comfort
- Tell us immediately if you feel unwell
