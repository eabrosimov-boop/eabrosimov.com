# Highland Argentina

## Arrival

**Buenos Aires → Salta**
- Land in the city of Guemes
- Settle into hotel
- Walk through the centre to a legend's monument
- Dinner with stories and local spirit

---

## Day 1: Salta

Immersion in the northwest. From city panoramas via funicular to history in museums.

**Route:** San Bernardo funicular → MAAM Museum → City restaurant → Purmamarca

---

## Day 2: Salinas Grandes

At 4000+ meters — snow, salt, endless sky. Jeeps to Hornocal at sunset. Live music at dinner.

**Route:** Mountains → Salt flats → Yellow mountain (14 colours) → Humahúaca

---

## Day 3: Canyons and Trails

Historic churches, mountain canyons, final shopping. Return to the city.

**Route:** Uquía → Quebrada de las Señoritas → Purmamarca → Salta
