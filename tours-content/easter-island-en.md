# Day 1. Welcome to the Edge of the World

We arrive at the mysterious island of Rapa Nui, surrounded by the endless Pacific Ocean.

At the foot of the aircraft stairs, we are greeted with flower necklaces — as the people of Polynesia have done for centuries.

Individual transfer to the hotel with a short sightseeing tour of the cozy village of Hanga Roa. This is the only city on the island that has preserved a special spirit: ocean on one side, volcano on the other, and everywhere — stone giants moai.

In the evening — free time. You can take a walk to the coast, listen to the sound of the waves and meet your first sunset at the edge of the world in complete silence.

After sunset — a shared dinner with traditional dishes of the island.

---

# Day 2. The Secrets of Moai and the Magic of Ancient Civilizations

We will see:
– Ahu Tongariki — a platform with 15 restored moai
– Volcano Rano Raraku — the "moai factory," where unfinished statues still lie
– Ahu Vaihu and Ahu Akahanga — mysterious ceremonial platforms
– Te Pito Kura — the mystical "navel of the earth"
– Ending the day — on the pristine Anakena beach by the ocean

Included: comfortable transport, certified guide and individual accompaniment

Duration: 6–7 hours

We will make a mandatory lunch stop, and dinner you will spend independently at any of the local restaurants (we will provide recommendations).

---

# Day 3. Sunrise in the Company of Moai and Farewell to the Island

We set out to meet the sunrise at the Tongariki platform — the most photogenic place on the island.

Imagine: the first rays of the sun breaking through the clouds, illuminating the silhouettes of ancient moai, and you sitting on a mat overlooking the ocean, enjoying a picnic breakfast with champagne.

Breakfast includes fresh fruits, pastries, drinks and — a glass of sparkling wine to gently celebrate the finale of this unique journey.

After — transfer to the airport and farewell to the island that will forever remain in your heart.
