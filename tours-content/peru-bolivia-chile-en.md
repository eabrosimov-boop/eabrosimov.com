# Day 1. Arrival in Lima

Arrival at Lima airport, Peru. Transfer and hotel check-in. After the journey — free time for rest and first walks around the city.

In the evening — meet the group and take a leisurely walk through evening Lima.

If desired, you can arrive a day earlier and visit sea lions with the possibility of swimming near them. This excursion is additional.

---

# Day 2. Peru's Capital - Lima

First day in Peru's capital — Lima, on the coast of the Pacific Ocean.

The city is famous for its historical and cultural attractions: colonial architecture, churches, museums, parks and gardens. We will explore the historic center, walk through the bohemian Barranco district and watch the sunset on the coast.

---

# Day 3. Flight to Cusco

In the morning we fly to Cusco. Upon arrival, we get acquainted with the capital of the ancient Inca empire: we walk through the historic center, see the Temple of the Sun Coricancha, the Santo Domingo Cathedral and the Cathedral. The tour is accompanied by a local guide.

Cusco is located at a height of about 3400 m. Light manifestations of altitude adaptation are possible. It is recommended to drink more water, avoid overexertion and alcohol. The hotel will offer coca leaf tea.

We will also visit the central market with its local color and exotic products.

---

# Day 4. Sacred Valley of the Incas

We travel in a comfortable bus to the Urubamba river valley — the cradle of Inca civilization. Along the way we visit the ancient city of Chinchero, then the settlement of Moray with a unique complex of more than 3000 terraces-pools.

The tour ends in Ollantaytambo, from where we take a train to Aguas Calientes. We will be accompanied by an ensemble in national costumes with music and dances.

---

# Day 5. Machu Picchu

At sunrise we head to Machu Picchu — the ancient city of the Incas at 2400 m altitude. Built in the 15th century, it is considered one of the seven wonders of the world.

We arrive early to enjoy the complex in the morning hours when there are still few tourists. At the first rays of dawn Machu Picchu is especially beautiful. Around 14:00 we leave for Cusco.

---

# Day 6. Rainbow Mountains

Early in the morning we depart by private transport to the Rainbow Mountains. The journey takes about 3-4 hours. Climb to Mount Vinicunca at about 5100 m, the climb and descent will take an average of 3-4 hours.

Important: the trek is at about 5000 m altitude. Even experienced travelers may feel strong strain. If you have contraindications, tell the guide immediately.

At the top opens an impressive sight of the Rainbow Mountains with their bright colored layers.

---

# Day 7. Lake Titicaca, Uros Islands

We travel by private transfer to the city of Puno to see one of South America's largest lakes — Lake Titicaca. We will cross by boat to the Uros islands, made of real reeds, and stay overnight there.

We will watch the sunset, meet the local people and dine on fresh trout.

Important: today we have a long journey — 6-8 hours.

---

# Day 8. Bolivia - La Paz

On this day we cross the Peru/Bolivia border to the highest city in the world, La Paz. Spanish culture intertwines with indigenous traditions, and colonial buildings coexist with skyscrapers.

We will ride on the world's largest urban cable car network Mi Teleférico and see the government palace. We will visit the famous Witch's Market, where real shamans and witches work.

---

# Day 9. Valley of Spirits

Early in the morning we get on a bus and go to the outskirts of La Paz to visit the Valley of Spirits and Valley of the Moon. Huge stone columns and bizarre lunar landscapes look like real sculptures.

We meet with a local shaman and learn about ancient traditions of the region. In the evening we have dinner in a meat restaurant with giant juicy steaks.

---

# Day 10. Locomotive Graveyard, Altiplano Plateau

After breakfast we fly to Uyuni. Upon arrival we board jeeps and begin a three-day tour.

First stop — locomotive graveyard with amazing views: blue sky and rusty locomotives left almost 100 years ago.

Next stop — Altiplano plateau with the world's largest salt flat. During the rainy season the salt flat reflects the sky, creating the illusion of flight.

Night in a salt hotel at 4400 meters altitude.

---

# Day 11. Laguna Colorado and Lagoons

We continue our journey across the Altiplano plateau. We will see colorful lagoons, Lake Colorado with a thousand flamingos, and visit the Valley of Stones.

The jeep tour includes three meals a day.

Night in a hotel on the salt flat at 4400 meters altitude.

---

# Day 12. Geysers, Salvador Dali Desert, San Pedro de Atacama

We rise before dawn to see geysers and bathe in hot springs. We visit several valleys and the Salvador Dali Desert with colorful mountains and surreal landscapes.

We see stone trees and possibly meet the mountain viscacha.

After crossing the border to Chile we reach San Pedro de Atacama — the driest desert in the world. At night — astronomical tour.

---

# Day 13. Flight to Santiago

In the morning, if you wish, you can walk around San Pedro de Atacama and then head to Chile's capital — Santiago.

After the flight we check into the hotel.

---

# Day 14. Valparaiso and Wineries

Tour to the town of Valparaiso on the Pacific coast. Graffiti on walls, colorful stairs and scatter of small houses on hilltops.

We will walk through the winding streets of the city, look into the port and ride the funicular. After lunch we go to the Casablanca valley with its vineyards.

We will finish the tour with a late lunch and tasting of Chilean wines.

---

# Day 15-17. Easter Island (optional)

Travelers who wish to continue can add an additional program to Easter Island — an intensive three-day journey through the island's most iconic sites.

Rapa Nui is a unique territory with no analogues in the world. The main symbol of the island is the moai stone statues, which inspire awe in travelers from around the world.

Other participants plan their departure on Day 15.
