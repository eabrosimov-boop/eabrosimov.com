# Day 1. Iguazu. Argentine Side

Today we fly to Iguazu, where we will see one of the largest waterfalls in the world from the Argentine side.

The bravest can take a boat ride and get very close to the waterfalls. Boat cost ~ $80

Believe me – the emotions are unforgettable.

---

# Day 2. Iguazu. Brazilian Side

We wake up and head to the Brazilian territory, where the most beautiful panoramic views of Iguazu Falls await.

Those interested can fly over the waterfall by helicopter. The cabin holds up to 5 passengers, and the cost per person is $150.

We'll also visit the bird park, which features 900 species of exotic parrots, flamingos, toucans and other birds in their natural habitat.

---

# Day 3. Buenos Aires. Departure

We return to Buenos Aires. We share our impressions, look at photos, hug and promise each other to go on another trip! Happy and tired, we depart to our homes.

Important! Book your return flight for a late departure on this day or the next day.

You can also fly further from Iguazu to explore more of Brazil, for example to Rio, or fly from São Paulo back home.
